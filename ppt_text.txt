1. ABSTRACT
25/03/25B.Tech           Mini Project :  2022-2026 3
• NutriAI is an intelligent nutrition and calorie estimation system that integrates computer
vision and dietary analytics to help users track their food intake.
• It uses the YOLOv8 model for real-time food classification and nutritional prediction,
trained on a custom dataset with PyTorch and Ultralytics YOLO.
• Advanced techniques like AdamW optimization, automatic mixed precision (AMP), and
data augmentation enhance model performance.
• The system includes a BMI and BMR calculator to estimate daily caloric needs and a
personalized diet planner for tailored meal recommendations.
• A food database lookup enables quick retrieval of nutritional information.
• Optimized for efficiency, NutriAI employs computer vision preprocessing and multi-
process execution.
• Experimental results confirm its accuracy in food recognition, calorie estimation, and diet
planning, making it a valuable tool for dietary assessment.
2. INTRODUCTION
• Dietary management can be a complex and overwhelming task, especially when 
trying to accurately track caloric intake and ensure proper nutrition. Many 
individuals struggle with maintaining balanced diets, understanding the nutritional
content of the food they consume, and creating personalized meal plans suited to 
their health goals.
• To address these challenges, we introduce NutriAI, an intelligent nutrition and 
calorie estimation system. NutriAI offers a personalized diet planning function, 
which tailors recommendations to users' unique needs based on factors such as 
age, weight, height, activity level, and dietary preferences.
• Built with cutting-edge technologies—including machine learning and a 
comprehensive food database—NutriAI empowers individuals to make informed 
dietary choices and achieve their health goals. Whether for weight loss, muscle 
gain, or maintenance, NutriAI transforms the way users approach nutrition and 
wellness.

3. EXISTING SYSTEM
B.Tech           Mini Project :  2020-2024 5
• Limited & Inaccurate Food Recognition
• Existing applications rely on manual food logging, which is time-consuming and 
error-prone.
• Many apps lack AI-powered food recognition, leading to inaccurate nutritional 
estimations.
• Generic & Non-Personalized Diet Plans
• Most platforms provide one-size-fits-all meal plans without considering individual 
calorie needs, preferences, or health goals.
• No real-time AI-driven customization based on user feedback or dietary habits.
• No Integrated BMI & BMR Calculation
• Users need separate tools to calculate BMI (Body Mass Index) and BMR (Basal 
Metabolic Rate), making the process fragmented.
• Lack of an all-in-one platform for health tracking and diet planning.
• Limited Food Database & Nutrient Lookup
• Many apps rely on incomplete or outdated food databases, missing regional or 
lesser-known food items.
• Users struggle to find accurate macro and micronutrient breakdowns for specific 
meals.

9. IMPLEMENTATION
• Technology Stack
• Frontend: HTML, CSS (for an interactive UI)
• Backend: Node.js with Express.js (to handle requests efficiently) and Flask 
(to run AI)
• Database: MongoDB (to store food data, user preferences, and meal plans)
• AI Inference:YOLOv8 (for food recognition) and OpenAI API (for diet 
recommendations)
• Key Features & Implementation Details
(a) AI-Powered Food Recognition
Users upload food images for analysis.YOLOv8 detects and classifies food 
items.Extracts nutritional values such as calories, macronutrients, and micronutrients.
(b) Personalized Diet Planning
Users input details like age, weight, height, and goals.AI generates a custom diet plan 
using BMI & BMR calculations.Real-time recommendations based on user 
preferences.
(c) Food Database & Nutrient Lookup
Stores a curated database of food items and their nutritional values.Users can search 
for specific food items to get detailed nutrient breakdowns.
